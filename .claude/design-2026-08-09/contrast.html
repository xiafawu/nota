What flatten actually costs

You picked 90 / 90 and the contrast holds. But measuring it showed flatten is not doing the work I said it was — push is. Same two panes, same push, only flatten differs. Look at whether the left one has more life in it, and whether the right one reads as paint.

Contrast moves 0.32 across the entire slider. Flatten is not what makes the text readable — pushing the band to 90 already narrows it to about ten points of luminance, so flatten is squeezing something that has already been squeezed. What it still charges full price for is the photographic quality: 15.5 points of luminance variation down to 1.5.

I had this backwards earlier and said flatten was “the whole trick”. The measurement says push is the whole trick and flatten is the tax.
