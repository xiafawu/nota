What actually differs

A · trailing column ships today

  • 288pt fixed column, transcript takes the rest at full height.
  • Nothing overlaps the text — the newest line is always clear.
  • Timer is 58pt: legible across a room, which is the point of it.
  • Marker list has somewhere to live.
  • Folds to a strip under 809pt; the marker list is the fold’s one loss.
  • ⌘L drawer opens over it (380pt, right edge). Known, accepted.

B · bottom glass bar new

  • Transcript gets the whole window. More text on screen at any width.
  • The bar floats over the last ~110pt — exactly where the newest line is.
  • Timer drops to ~22pt to fit the bar height.
  • No home for the marker list; markers become a popover or go inline.
  • No fold needed — the bar is the same at every width.
  • Needs a scrim under it or the glass sits on moving colour and shimmers.

What centring buys, and what it costs. A 620pt measure is about 72 characters at 15.5pt — a comfortable read, and it holds at every window width, so there is no fold and no strip form. The cost is that a wide window is mostly ground: at 1400pt the transcript uses 44% of it. That is a deliberate reading surface rather than a filled one, and it is the same call Apple Music makes with lyrics.

Three things the bar now owes

  • The markers need a home. The column had a list at the bottom; the bar has no room for one. Cheapest answer: a marker draws a hairline into the transcript at the point it was set, and the bar shows a count that opens a popover. That is better than the old list either way — a timestamp beside the words it marks means something, a timestamp in a sidebar does not.
  • The scrim is load-bearing, not decoration. Glass over a moving ground shimmers. The bar sits on a soft vertical fade (already in this mock) so the colour under it is calm even while the field keeps flowing everywhere else.
  • The timer lost its size. 58pt in the column, ~22pt in the bar. It is still mono and tabular, and it is still the most legible thing in the bar, but "legible across a room" is gone. If that mattered, the answer is a hover or click that blooms the bar to a taller card — not a permanent column.
