How the noise is generated / cost\n\nIt is a SwiftUI **`Canvas`** \u2014 immediate-mode vector drawing, not an `Image`, not a Metal shader, not `.background(ImagePaint)`.\n\nCost per render pass: `columns = 28`, `rows = max(1, Int(size.height / size.width * 28))` \u2014 so it is **aspect-derived**, roughly 28\u00d716 \u2248 448 dots on a 16:9 window, ~28\u00d718 \u2248 500 on a typical 780\u00d7600 window.
